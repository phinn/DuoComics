Support

English

Contact: phinn@outlook.com

  • Import: tap + in the library and pick a CBZ / ZIP file. Pages are sorted naturally (2 before 10).
  • Reading modes: Single page, dual-page spread, and webtoon vertical scroll. Toggle right-to-left for manga in Settings.
  • Reading position: restored automatically when you reopen a comic.
  • Privacy: everything stays on your device. No account, no tracking.
